## Answer

Anterior cervical corpectomy with fusion is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria have been met [4]:

1.  **Procedure Indication:** Complete corpectomy or partial corpectomy (defined as removal of at least one-half of the vertebral body, not for resection of osteophytes alone) is performed for ANY of the following conditions [4]:
    *   Infection [4]
    *   Trauma [4]
    *   Tumor [4]
    *   Compression at or behind the level of the vertebral body [4]

2.  **Fusion Requirement:** Anterior cervical corpectomy must be performed with a cervical fusion due to the iatrogenic instability of the corpectomy procedure [4].

3.  **ACDF Criteria:** Anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF) criteria have been met in the applicable procedure-specific section [4]. The context lists the following sections but does not provide the specific criteria within them:

    *   CMM-601.7: Adjacent Segment Disease [4]
