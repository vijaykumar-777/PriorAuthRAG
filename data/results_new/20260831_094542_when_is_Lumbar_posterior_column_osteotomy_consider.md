# Query: when is Lumbar posterior column osteotomy considered medically necessary

*Generated: 2026-08-31 09:45:42 | mode=keyword | alpha=0.5 | top_k=10*

## Answer

Lumbar posterior column osteotomy (PCO) is considered medically necessary (in addition to fusion) when ALL of the following criteria have been met [3]:

*   It is performed for non-fixed deformity requiring 5° to 10° of correction (SPO) per spinal segment with EITHER of the following [3]:
    *   Lumbar sagittal plane deformities where sagittal vertical axis (SVA) is >8cm or pelvic incidence-lumbar lordosis (PI-LL) is >15° [3].
    *   Larger coronal deformities where there is limited flexibility and the Cobb angle is >30° [3].
*   Posterior column osteotomy is limited to a maximum of four (4) posterior column osteotomies performed in the apex of the deformity per correction surgery [3].
    *   **Criteria exception:** There is no limit to posterior column osteotomies for correction of Scheuermann's kyphosis as this deformity is long, gradual, rounded, and amendable to more than four (4) posterior column osteotomies [3].
*   Criteria have been met in the applicable procedure-specific section [3]:
    *   CMM-609.3: Pediatric Spinal Deformity [3].
    *   CMM-609.4: Lumbar Fusion (Arthrodesis) with Decompression (Indirect or Direct) [3].
    *   CMM-609.5: Lumbar Fusion (Arthrodesis) without Decompression [3].
    *   CMM-609.6: Adjacent Segment Disease [3].
    *   CMM-609.7: Lumbar Fusion (with or without Decompression) Following Failed Disc Arthroplasty Surgery [3].

#### Posterior Column Osteotomy (PCO)

Lumbar posterior column osteotomy (PCO) (i.e., Smith-Peterson osteotomy [SPO] or Ponte osteotomy) is considered medically necessary (in addition to fusion) when ALL of the following criteria have been met:

- Performed for non-fixed deformity requiring 5° to 10° of correction (SPO) per spinal segment with EITHER of the following:

- lumbar sagittal plane deformities where sagittal vertical axis (SVA) is &gt;8cm or pelvic incidence-lumbar lordosis (PI-LL) is &gt;15°

- larger coronal deformities where there is limited flexibility and the Cobb angle is &gt;30°

- Posterior column osteotomy is limited to a maximum of four (4) posterior column osteotomies performed in the apex of the deformity per correction surgery.

- Criteria exception : There is no limit to posterior column osteotomies for correction of Scheuermann's kyphosis as this deformity is long, gradual, rounded, and amendable to more than four (4) posterior column osteotomies

- Criteria have been met in the applicable procedure-specific section:

- CMM-609.3: Pediatric Spinal Deformity

- CMM-609.4: Lumbar Fusion (Arthrodesis) with Decompression (Indirect or Direct)
