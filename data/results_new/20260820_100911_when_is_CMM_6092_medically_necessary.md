Based on the provided context, Section CMM-609.2 (Osteotomy) contains two distinct subsections with specific criteria for medical necessity: **Posterior Column Osteotomy (PCO)** and **Three-Column Osteotomy**.

**Posterior Column Osteotomy (PCO)**
Lumbar posterior column osteotomy (PCO) (i.e., Smith-Peterson osteotomy [SPO] or Ponte osteotomy) is considered medically necessary (in addition to fusion) when ALL of the following criteria have been met: [9]
- Performed for non-fixed deformity requiring 5° to 10° of correction (SPO) per spinal segment with EITHER of the following: [9]
    - lumbar sagittal plane deformities where sagittal vertical axis (SVA) is >8cm or pelvic incidence-lumbar lordosis (PI-LL) is >15° [9]
    - larger coronal deformities where there is limited flexibility and the Cobb angle is >30° [9]
- Posterior column osteotomy is limited to a maximum of four (4) posterior column osteotomies performed in the apex of the deformity per correction surgery. [9]
- **Criteria exception**: There is no limit to posterior column osteotomies for correction of Scheuermann's kyphosis as this deformity is long, gradual, rounded, and amendable to more than four (4) posterior column osteotomies [9]
- Criteria have been met in the applicable procedure-specific section: [9]

    - CMM-609.4: Lumbar Fusion (Arthrodesis) with Decompression (Indirect or Direct) [9]
    - CMM-609.5: Lumbar Fusion (Arthrodesis) without Decompression [9]

    - CMM-609.7: Lumbar Fusion (with or without Decompression) Following Failed Disc Arthroplasty Surgery [9]
    - CMM-609.8: Repeat Lumbar Fusion (Arthrodesis) at the Same Level [9]

**Three-Column Osteotomy**
Lumbar three-column osteotomy (i.e., pedicle subtraction osteotomy (PSO) or vertebral column resection [VCR]) is considered medically necessary (in addition to fusion) when ALL of the following criteria have been met: [10]
- Performed for EITHER of the following: [10]
