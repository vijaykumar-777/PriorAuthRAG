# Query: when is ACDF not medically necessary

*Generated: 2026-08-19 15:47:01 | mode=hybrid | alpha=0.5 | top_k=5*

## Answer

Based on the provided context, Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) is considered **not medically necessary** in the following situations:

*   When performed without meeting the criteria in the **General Guidelines** (when applicable for urgent/emergent conditions) and the criteria in the applicable procedure-specific sections (initial fusion, corpectomy, repeat fusion, adjacent segment disease, or fusion following failed disc arthroplasty) [1].
*   When performed for **any other reason**, including but not limited to:
    *   Chronic non-specific neck or arm pain of unknown etiology [1].
    *   Cervical degenerative disc disease without radiculopathy or myelopathy [1].
*   When anterior cervical discectomy/corpectomy is performed **alone** (i.e., without a cervical fusion) [1].
