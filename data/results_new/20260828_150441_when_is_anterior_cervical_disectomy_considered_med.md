## Answer

Based on the provided context, specifically section CMM-601.3, anterior cervical discectomy is considered medically necessary under the following conditions:

- Anterior cervical discectomy is considered medically necessary when performed with a cervical fusion [6].
- Anterior cervical discectomy must be performed with a cervical fusion due to the iatrogenic instability/increased disc degeneration of the anterior cervical discectomy procedure [6].
- Anterior cervical discectomy and fusion criteria have been met in the applicable procedure-specific section: CMM-601.4: Initial Primary Anterior Cervical Discectomy and Fusion (ACDF) [6].

- Anterior cervical discectomy and fusion criteria have been met in the applicable procedure-specific section: CMM-601.7: Adjacent Segment Disease [6].
- Anterior cervical discectomy and fusion criteria have been met in the applicable procedure-specific section: CMM-601.8: ACDF Following Failed Cervical Disc Arthroplasty Surgery [6].

Additionally, the context notes that anterior cervical discectomy performed alone (i.e., without a cervical fusion) is considered not medically necessary [1].
