## Answer

Based on the provided context, the following documentation and criteria are required for Arthrodesis (specifically Lumbar Fusion):

*   **Imaging Findings:** Imaging findings noted in the applicable procedure section(s) are required. [1]
*   **Nicotine-Free Status Documentation:** Documentation of nicotine-free status is required, validated by EITHER of the following: [2]
    *   The individual is a never-smoker. [2]
    *   The individual has refrained from smoking, use of smokeless tobacco products, and/or nicotine replacement therapy for at least six (6) weeks prior to planned surgery, validated by objective cotinine testing methods (serum, urinary, or saliva) verified as within the normal range for the testing method and lab at which the test was performed. [2]
*   **Exceptions for Urgent/Emergent Conditions:** The following criteria are NOT required for confirmed urgent/emergent conditions: [1]
    *   Provider-directed non-surgical management. [1]
    *   Proof of smoking cessation. [1]
    *   Absence of unmanaged significant mental and/or behavioral health disorders (e.g., major depressive disorder, chronic pain syndrome, secondary gain, opioid and alcohol use disorders). [1]
    *   Time frame for repeat procedure. [1]
*   **Exceptions for Intra-Operative Instability:** When instability is created and/or identified intra-operatively, the above imaging criteria are NOT required. [2]
