## 2. Locked Decisions

### Decision 2: 10 Selected CPT Codes
The 10 representative CPT codes covering core primary procedures, add-on codes, corpectomies, osteotomies, and instrumentation:
1. **`22551`** — Anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF), single interspace

3. **`22554`** — Arthrodesis, anterior interbody, cervical below C2
4. **`63075`** — Discectomy, anterior, with decompression, single interspace, cervical
5. **`63076`** — Discectomy, anterior, cervical, each additional interspace (Add-on)
6. **`63081`** — Vertebral corpectomy, anterior, cervical, single segment
7. **`63082`** — Vertebral corpectomy, cervical, each additional segment (Add-on)
8. **`22220`** — Osteotomy of spine, anterior approach, single interspace, cervical
9. **`22845`** — Anterior instrumentation; 2 to 3 vertebral bodies
10. **`22853`** — Insertion of interbody spinal biomechanical device with bone graft / cage

### Decision 3: Output File & Path
- **File:** `Policy_Ground_Truth.xlsx`
- **Directory:** `data/ground_truth/` (full path: `data/ground_truth/Policy_Ground_Truth.xlsx`)
- **Format:** Excel `.xlsx` with openpyxl/pandas formatting, headers frozen, column widths auto-fitted.

### Decision 4: Row Granularity & Schema
Strictly follow the 15-column schema required by `Task Description and Checklist.md` (lines 49–80):
- **Granularity:** **One row per medical-necessity criterion** (not combined into single cell blocks).
- **Columns:**
  1. `Payer` (e.g. `Cigna / EviCore`)
  2. `Policy ID` (`CMM-601`)
  3. `CPT` (5-digit code)
  4. `Procedure` (Official procedure title)
  5. `Condition/Indication` (e.g. `Cervical Radiculopathy`, `Cervical Myelopathy`, `Adjacent Segment Disease`)
  6. `PA Required` (`Yes` or `Add On`)
  7. `Criterion ID` (e.g. `C1`, `C2`, `C3`...)
  8. `Medical-Necessity Criterion` (Concrete requirement statement)
  9. `Logic` (`AND` vs `OR`)
  10. `Threshold` (e.g. `≥6 weeks`, `>6 months`, `—`)
  11. `Documentation Required` (e.g. `Physician note`, `MRI report`, `Flexion/extension X-rays`, `Cotinine lab test`)
  12. `Exception/Exclusion` (e.g. `Contraindications to conservative therapy`, `Severe progressive neurologic deficit`)
  13. `Page` (Exact page number in `Cigna_ACDF.pdf`)
  14. `Section` (Exact section title in TOC hierarchy)
  15. `Source Text` (Exact verbatim quote from policy PDF)

### Decision 5: Ground Truth Verification Guarantee
- **Rule:** Zero LLM generation for ground truth.
- Every criterion statement, threshold, page citation, and section heading must be verified directly against `Cigna_ACDF.pdf`.
