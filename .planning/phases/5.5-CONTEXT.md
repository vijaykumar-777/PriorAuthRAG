## 2. Locked Decisions

### Decision 1: Root-Cause Classification Taxonomy
All 25 failed cases will be diagnosed and mapped into the 6 checklist categories:
1. **Policy-Understanding & Boolean Logic Errors:** Misinterpreting "at least TWO of THREE" as requiring all three modalities (e.g. `SYN-001`).
2. **Missing Evidence & Hallucination Errors:** Approving (`MET`) cases where mandatory imaging or PT reports were absent (`UNKNOWN`) (e.g. `SYN-003`, `SYN-011`).
3. **Clinical Reasoning & Boundary Errors:** Disregarding strict temporal thresholds (e.g. accepting 4 weeks of PT instead of mandatory 6 weeks in `SYN-012`).
4. **Conflict Detection Errors:** Failing to detect discordant findings between physician exam notes and radiology reports (e.g. `SYN-007`).
5. **Hierarchical Retrieval Errors:** Omission of multi-level policy criteria during scoping.
6. **Citation Provenance Errors:** Inaccurate section or page references.

### Decision 2: Hardening Strategy in Test Harness
Update the Stage 2 clinical adjudication engine in `scripts/run_pa_test_harness.py`:
- **Explicit Boolean Logic Guidance:** Explicitly instruct the model on disjunctive criteria ("at least N of M") so it never penalizes patients for omitting optional alternative modalities once the count threshold is met.
- **Zero-Tolerance Document Absence Rule:** Instruct the model that if an imaging report or PT note is absent from the submitted files, the criterion cannot be satisfied via secondary mentions in physician notes alone; it must be flagged as `UNKNOWN` / `NOT MET`.
- **Cross-Modal Conflict Checking:** Explicitly instruct the model to compare physical exam findings against radiology reports. If physician notes claim severe stenosis/compression but radiology reports indicate normal/mild findings without compression, force `CONFLICT`.
- **Strict Boundary Enforcement:** Enforce mathematical boundary compliance (duration $\ge$ 6 weeks).

### Decision 3: Re-Run & Regression Suite
- Re-run the batch evaluation across cases to verify the fix and measure the before/after accuracy delta.
- Build `scripts/run_regression_suite.py` containing a curated regression benchmark covering all 12 checklist archetypes:
  - All criteria met
  - One criterion failed
  - Missing information
  - Boundary conditions
  - AND logic
  - OR logic
  - Exceptions
  - Wrong indication
  - Missing documents
  - Conflicting documents
  - Multiple clinical documents
  - Multiple CPTs
